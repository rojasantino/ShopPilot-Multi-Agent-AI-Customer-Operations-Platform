# Shipping Policy
## Delivery times
Standard delivery within the UAE takes 2 to 4 business days. Express delivery takes 1 business day in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

## Shipping cost
Shipping is free for orders above 200 AED. Orders below 200 AED have a flat shipping fee of 15 AED.

## Late deliveries
If your order arrives later than the expected delivery date, contact support and we will review a shipping fee refund or a goodwill credit.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link by email once the order status changes to shipped.
