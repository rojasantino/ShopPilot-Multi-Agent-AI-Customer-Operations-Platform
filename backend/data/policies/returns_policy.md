# Returns Policy
ShopPilot Store accepts returns within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, you can return it within the 30-day return window. Send a photo of the damage so our team can review the refund request. Refunds for damaged items are approved by a support specialist.

## Final sale items
Items marked as final sale or clearance cannot be returned or refunded unless they arrive damaged.

## Refund timing
After a refund is approved by our team, the money is returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds are never issued automatically; every refund request is reviewed by a human agent.
